LeetBot: Your AI-Powered LeetCode Companion

🚀 Supercharge your LeetCode journey with LeetBot, the ultimate AI assistant designed to help you MASTER problems, not just solve them.

Stuck on a hard graph problem? Need a hint without spoiling the solution? Want to visualize a complex algorithm? LeetBot integrates seamlessly into your LeetCode workflow, providing intelligent guidance, interactive visualizations, and a beautiful, modern interface.

💡 WHY LEETBOT?
LeetBot isn't just another "answer generator." It acts as a private tutor, helping you build intuition and problem-solving skills. Whether you're a beginner learning data structures or a pro optimizing for time complexity, LeetBot adapts to your needs.

✨ KEY FEATURES:

🧠 Intelligent AI Modes
• Tutor Mode: Get deep, conceptual explanations of the problem. Understand the "why" and "how" behind the solution.
• Hint Mode: Receive subtle, step-by-step nudges. Perfect for when you're stuck but want to reach the solution yourself.
• Planner Mode: Collaboratively design a solution strategy. Discuss brute-force approaches, optimizations, and edge cases before writing a single line of code.

📈 Interactive Graph Visualizations
• Auto-Generated Diagrams: Instantly turn complex logic into clear flowcharts, trees, and sequence diagrams using Mermaid.js.
• Zoom & Pan: Navigate large, complex visualizations with intuitive pinch-to-zoom and drag controls.
• Responsive Design: Diagrams automatically scale for maximum readability.

🪟 Stunning Glassmorphic UI
• Floating Window: A beautiful, non-intrusive overlay that sits on top of your problem page.
• Jelly Physics: Enjoy a delightful user experience with smooth, springy window interactions.
• Fully Customizable: Resize, move, and snap the window anywhere. It remembers your preferences!
• Theme Sync: Automatically adapts to your LeetCode theme (Light/Dark mode) for a native feel.

🔒 Privacy-First & Secure
• Your Keys, Your Control: LeetBot uses your own API key (Gemini/AI). Keys are stored securely in your browser's local storage and never sent to our servers.
• Open Source: Transparent and community-driven. Check out our code on GitHub!

🛠️ HOW TO USE:
1. Install the extension.
2. Open any LeetCode problem page.
3. Click the floating "Brain" icon to open LeetBot.
4. Enter your API key in the settings.
5. Choose your mode (Tutor, Hint, Planner) and start chatting!

• Developers brushing up on algorithms.
• Anyone who wants to visualize and understand code better.
