# Privacy Policy for LeetBot

**Last Updated: November 24, 2025**

LeetBot ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome browser extension.

## 1. Information We Collect

LeetBot is designed to be privacy-first. We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide our services.

### A. User-Provided Information
- **API Keys:** To function, LeetBot requires you to provide your own API key (e.g., Google Gemini). This key is stored locally on your device within your browser's secure storage (`chrome.storage.local`). It is **never** sent to our servers.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
- **Page Content:** When you activate LeetBot on a LeetCode problem page, the extension reads the problem title, description, and your current code snippet. This information is required to generate relevant hints and solutions.
- **Extension Usage Data:** We may store local preferences, such as your selected theme (Light/Dark) or preferred AI mode, to improve your user experience.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the sole purpose of providing the LeetBot service:
- **To Generate AI Responses:** The problem description and code snippets you provide are sent directly from your browser to the AI provider (e.g., Google Gemini API) using your personal API key.
- **To Maintain Preferences:** Your settings are stored locally to persist your configuration across sessions.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

- **AI Providers:** The content of the LeetCode problem and your code is transmitted to the AI provider (Google Gemini) to generate responses. This transmission is subject to the privacy policy of the respective AI provider.
- **No Third-Party Sales:** We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others.
- **No Server Storage:** LeetBot does not have a backend server. All processing happens locally in your browser or directly between your browser and the AI API.

## 4. Data Security

- **Local Storage:** Sensitive data, such as your API key, is stored in `chrome.storage.local`, which is sandboxed to the extension.
- **Direct Communication:** Communication with the AI API occurs directly from your browser. We do not act as a middleman.

## 5. Your Choices

- **API Key Management:** You can update or remove your API key at any time through the extension's settings.
- **Uninstall:** You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the extension from your browser.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
